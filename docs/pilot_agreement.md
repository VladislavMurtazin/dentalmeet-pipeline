## 2. Qualified Meeting definition

A **"Qualified Meeting"** meets **ALL** of the following:

1. The meeting actually took place (not a no-show or cancellation).  
2. The meeting duration was at least **15 minutes**.  
3. The attendee holds a decision-making position (**Owner, CEO, Founder, President, Practice Manager, or Director**).  
4. The attendee’s dental practice is located within the Client’s **target geographic area** (as specified in the onboarding form / ICP).  
5. The attendee represents a **legitimate dental practice** (not a competitor, student, or researcher).  
6. The attendee has **not** previously been booked for a meeting in the **same calendar month** (no duplicates).

Meetings that do not meet ALL criteria are **not** billed.  
Provider will supply a detailed report of all meetings booked, which were qualified, and the reason for any disqualification.

## 4. Refund / no-delivery policy (Pilot)

- If Provider delivers **fewer than 5** qualified meetings in the pilot window, Client pays **only** for qualified meetings delivered (`× $150`), subject to the `$750` cap.  
- If **zero** qualified meetings are delivered, Client pays **$0**.  
- Setup fees on Repeat engagements are non-refundable once outreach has launched, except where Provider fails to launch within the agreed window after upfront payment is confirmed.
